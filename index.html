Best Model Analysis

The Best Model identified during training was ResNet-18 using the Adam Optimizer (LR: 0.001).

By optimizing training with 5 Epochs and Memory Pinning, the model achieved a peak Test Accuracy of 99.21% on the MNIST dataset.

This configuration outperformed ResNet-50 in both convergence speed and computational efficiency, making it the most effective choice for the MNIST classification task.

Key Learnings

  • Hyperparameter Impact: I observed that Adam provides significantly more stable convergence than SGD, especially at lower learning rates where SGD accuracy dropped below the 80% threshold.
  • Hardware Optimization: Implementing USE_AMP=True and pin_memory=True demonstrated how to minimize training latency on GPU resources.
  • Architecture Choice: For 28x28 grayscale images, I learned that a deeper model like ResNet-50 is often "overkill" and can lead to slower training without accuracy gains.
